# DEFORMED

*Distance is a timetable.*

**Spain from [City]**: every city has its own Spain.

An experimental cartography of Spain where distance is replaced by
railway travel time. Pick an origin city: it stays fixed while the rest
of the territory deforms according to direct train journey times.

## Status
MVP (V0) in planning. Direct connections only, 18 regional capitals.

## Research question
How does the spatial representation of Spain change when geographic
distance is replaced by rail travel time from a given origin?

## Scope (V0)
- 18 autonomous community capitals
- Direct rail connections only (no transfers, frequency or waiting time)
- Origin city anchored; global deformation of the map
- Island capitals shown as cities without rail connection

## What this map cannot tell you
Passenger experience, train availability, frequency, ticket price,
accessibility, intra-city travel or services missing from the sources.
